Real-World Example: Research Paper Summarization

The Goal

A university wants a system that can summarize a 20-page research paper. The model must find relationships between different passages, pull out the important information, and write a coherent summary.

This summarizer runs in your browser: it splits the text into tokens, scores the important words, and picks the sentences that carry the most weight. A real Transformer such as BART or Pegasus learns these relationships from data and writes new sentences instead of copying them.

Key Point

Attention lets each token take in information from other relevant positions in the sequence.
